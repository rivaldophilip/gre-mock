## Reading skills for the 13 RC slots (arg-* on argument slots)

- **rc-function** ×2 (review, wrong on a question testing it; wildcard, wrong on a question testing it): Why a sentence, example or paragraph is there (to concede, illustrate, qualify, contrast). Traps: an accurate paraphrase that misstates its role.
- **rc-structure** (review, wrong on a question testing it): How the passage or a paragraph is built (claim then counterexample, two views then a synthesis). Traps: one move out of order or missing.
- **rc-main-idea** (review, wrong on a question testing it): The passage's central claim, or why the author wrote it. Traps: too narrow (one paragraph's point), too broad, right topic with the wrong verb ("refute" vs "qualify").
- **rc-inference** (review, wrong on a question testing it): What must be true or is most strongly supported. Traps: plausible but unsupported, true in the world but not in the passage, extreme wording.
- **rc-select-verify** (review, wrong on a question testing it): Each statement checked independently against the text (select-all or EXCEPT). Traps: a true-in-the-world but unsupported choice; one half-right choice.
- **rc-application** (review, right but slow): Which new situation parallels, would be explained by, or would be judged by the passage's idea.
- **rc-attitude** ×2 (new, right but slow): The author's stance and degree of certainty toward a view or person. Traps: tone too strong ("scathing") or the opposite polarity; the author vs a view the author reports.
- **rc-vocab-context** (new, covered, no weakness): What a word or phrase means as used here. Traps: the word's common sense where the passage uses a secondary one.
- **rc-detail** (new, right but slow): What the passage says explicitly. Traps: right words from the wrong part of the passage, distortions of degree or scope.
- **arg-assumption** (review, wrong on a question testing it): The unstated premise the argument depends on (negation test). Traps: sufficient but not necessary assumptions, too-strong wording.
- **arg-role** (new, never tested): What part a highlighted statement plays: conclusion, premise, counter-premise, intermediate conclusion.

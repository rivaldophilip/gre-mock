## Reading skills for the 13 RC slots (arg-* on argument slots)

Write each slot's question as its variant (the form of the skill earlier mocks used least); a skill drawn twice gets two. Put a variant on a slot whose format fits it (an EXCEPT variant on a single-answer rc slot).

- **rc-select-verify** ×2 (review, wrong on a question testing it; wildcard, wrong on a question testing it): Each statement checked independently against the text (select-all or EXCEPT). Traps: a true-in-the-world but unsupported choice; one half-right choice. Variants: select-all detail (which of these the passage mentions); select-all inference about a claim the passage makes.
- **rc-function** (review, wrong on a question testing it): Why a sentence, example or paragraph is there (to concede, illustrate, qualify, contrast). Traps: an accurate paraphrase that misstates its role. Variant: the role of one sentence in the passage as a whole.
- **rc-inference** (review, wrong on a question touching it): What must be true or is most strongly supported, including what a scholar or group the passage cites believes or would agree with. Traps: plausible but unsupported, true in the world but not in the passage, extreme wording, the author's view given for a cited party's. Variant: a conclusion that needs two sentences combined.
- **rc-vocab-context** (review, wrong on a question testing it): What a word or phrase means as used here. Traps: the word's common sense where the passage uses a secondary one. Variant: a common word used in a secondary sense.
- **rc-structure** (review, wrong on a question testing it): How the passage or a paragraph is built (claim then counterexample, two views then a synthesis). Traps: one move out of order or missing. Variant: how one paragraph relates to the one before it.
- **rc-main-idea** (review, wrong on a question testing it): The passage's central claim, or why the author wrote it. Traps: too narrow (one paragraph's point), too broad, right topic with the wrong verb ("refute" vs "qualify"). Variant: what the passage is primarily concerned with (topic plus stance).
- **rc-application** ×2 (new, right but slow): Which new situation parallels, would be explained by, or would be judged by the passage's idea. Variants: a case the passage's principle would judge; evidence that would support or weaken a hypothesis the passage describes.
- **rc-attitude** (new, wrong on a question touching it): The author's stance and degree of certainty toward a view or person. Traps: tone too strong ("scathing") or the opposite polarity; a view the author only reports, or floats as speculation, read as one the author holds. Variant: the author's attitude toward a school or view the passage reports.
- **rc-detail** (new, right but slow): What the passage says explicitly. Traps: right words from the wrong part of the passage, distortions of degree or scope. Variant: a fact stated in a different paragraph from the stem's keywords.
- **arg-complete** (review, covered, no weakness): Which statement most logically fills the blank that ends the passage, often after "since" or "because", so the key is the missing premise that makes the conclusion follow. Traps: a choice that restates the conclusion, or supports a neighbouring claim. Variant: the missing premise after since ___ or because ___.
- **arg-flaw** (new, covered, no weakness): The reasoning error or technique (sampling, causation from correlation, equivocation). Variant: the method of reasoning the argument uses.

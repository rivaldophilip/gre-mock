## Reading skills for the 13 RC slots (arg-* on argument slots)

Write each slot's question as its variant (the form of the skill earlier mocks used least); a skill drawn twice gets two. Put a variant on a slot whose format fits it (an EXCEPT variant on a single-answer rc slot).

- **rc-select-verify** ×2 (review, wrong on a question testing it; wildcard, wrong on a question testing it): Each statement checked independently against the text (select-all or EXCEPT). Traps: a true-in-the-world but unsupported choice; one half-right choice. Variants: single-answer EXCEPT item, each choice checked against the text; select-all detail (which of these the passage mentions).
- **rc-function** (review, wrong on a question testing it): Why a sentence, example or paragraph is there (to concede, illustrate, qualify, contrast). Traps: an accurate paraphrase that misstates its role. Variant: the significance of a study or finding the passage mentions.
- **rc-inference** (review, wrong on a question touching it): What must be true or is most strongly supported, including what a scholar or group the passage cites believes or would agree with. Traps: plausible but unsupported, true in the world but not in the passage, extreme wording, the author's view given for a cited party's. Variant: a conclusion that needs two sentences combined.
- **rc-vocab-context** (review, wrong on a question testing it): What a word or phrase means as used here. Traps: the word's common sense where the passage uses a secondary one. Variant: a term the author uses in a special or narrowed sense.
- **rc-main-idea** ×2 (review, wrong on a question testing it; new, wrong on a question testing it): The passage's central claim, or why the author wrote it. Traps: too narrow (one paragraph's point), too broad, right topic with the wrong verb ("refute" vs "qualify"). Variants: the central issue or question the passage addresses; the main point of one paragraph.
- **rc-structure** (review, wrong on a question testing it): How the passage or a paragraph is built (claim then counterexample, two views then a synthesis). Traps: one move out of order or missing. Variant: the organization of the whole passage.
- **rc-detail** (new, right but slow): What the passage says explicitly. Traps: right words from the wrong part of the passage, distortions of degree or scope. Variant: all of the following are stated EXCEPT.
- **rc-application** (new, wrong on a question testing it): Which new situation parallels, would be explained by, or would be judged by the passage's idea. Variant: an assumption the author's argument in the passage depends on.
- **rc-attitude** (new, wrong on a question touching it): The author's stance and degree of certainty toward a view or person. Traps: tone too strong ("scathing") or the opposite polarity; a view the author only reports, or floats as speculation, read as one the author holds. Variant: how firmly the author commits to a claim (asserted vs speculative).
- **arg-weaken** (review, wrong on a question testing it): New information that most undermines the conclusion, usually by attacking an assumption. Traps: out-of-scope facts, attacks on a premise rather than the link. Variant: a reason a plan or prediction will fail.
- **arg-conclusion** (new, covered, no weakness): What the statements most strongly support, the point the passage emphasizes, or which statement the information directly contradicts. Traps: a claim one notch too strong, or one the passage merely does not mention. Variant: which statement the information directly contradicts.
